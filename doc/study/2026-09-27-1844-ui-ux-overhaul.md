# Study: UI/UX Overhaul
- **Date:** 2026-09-27 18:44
- **Request:** Turn the scaffolded templates into a polished, responsive, minimalist Tailwind UI. **Templates (and UI-only template tags) only**: no changes to models, views, URLs, or backend behavior. Deliverables:
  - A design system in `base.html`.
  - A **3-column social layout** (sidebar nav · feed · "Who to follow"/Trending) that collapses to one column with a **bottom nav** on mobile.
  - A **SaaS-style advertiser layout** (sidebar · top header · metric cards and tables).
  - Reusable `{% include %}` components (post card, ad card, buttons, form inputs).
  - Direction: *clean, minimalist, modern; lots of whitespace; `rounded-xl`/`2xl`; subtle shadows; Inter; light mode; neutral gray/white with one vibrant brand color*, plus 3 mockup images and 1 palette image.

## Intended Outcome
Every page looks like one coherent product on a phone (~390 px) and on a desktop (≥ 1280 px), for all three audiences (visitors, regular users, advertisers). **Every behavior stays the same:** the same URLs, the same forms and fields, the same permission results, and the same fixed query counts except for a documented, constant increase from the new sidebar widgets. All existing tests pass.

**Acceptance criteria**
- [ ] Desktop (`lg`, ≥ 1024 px), regular user: left sidebar nav, center feed, right rail with *Who to follow* and *Trending*. Between 1024 and 1279 px the right rail is hidden and the layout is 2 columns.
- [ ] Mobile (< 1024 px), regular user: one column, a sticky top bar, and a **fixed bottom nav** (Feed · People · Post · Profile). The page content isn't hidden behind the bottom nav.
- [ ] Desktop, advertiser: a fixed left sidebar (Dashboard, New campaign, campaign list), a top header (page title, company, account menu), metric cards, and a data table.
- [ ] Mobile, advertiser: the sidebar collapses into a top header with a disclosure menu, and the tables scroll horizontally inside their cards.
- [ ] Visitor pages (landing, login, both signups, 403) use a centered, branded layout.
- [ ] Every form renders through one field component: label, input, help text, and errors, styled consistently, including error states.
- [ ] No JavaScript is needed. Menus use native `<details>`.
- [ ] The backend is unchanged: `git diff` shows no changes to `models.py`, `views.py`, `urls.py`, `forms.py`, or settings.

## Reconciling the design inputs
| Input | What it says |
|---|---|
| Written direction | Minimal, lots of whitespace, `rounded-xl`/`2xl`, subtle shadows, **Inter**, light mode, **neutral gray/white + one vibrant primary** |
| Mockups ("Talk Sense") | Soft pink/lavender atmosphere, white rounded cards floating on it, **vibrant pink/magenta accent** (e.g. "SMM model", notification badges), pill-shaped chips, avatar row, a 4-item bottom tab bar with icons, serif display headings |
| Palette | `#DEF2C8` Tea Green · `#C5DAC1` Tea Green · `#BCD0C7` Ash Grey · `#A9B2AC` Ash Grey · `#898980` Grey Olive, a desaturated **sage** neutral ramp |

**Synthesis (a direction decision, flagged for the Gardener):**
- **The neutrals come from the palette.** Page backgrounds, borders, muted surfaces, and secondary text use a sage-tinted gray ramp built from the five swatches, extended with a near-white and a dark olive for readable text. The UI stays "neutral gray/white", with a hint of green warmth instead of cold slate.
- **The one vibrant primary is the mockups' magenta-pink** (`#E0266F`, "brand"). It's used for primary buttons, the active nav item, the like heart, links, and focus rings. Pink and sage are complementary, so the accent stands out clearly against the neutrals.
- **The mockups' atmosphere** is brought in lightly: a very soft pink/sage blurred gradient behind the landing page and auth cards only. App pages stay on the flat neutral background, as the written direction asks.
- **Font:** **Inter** everywhere, as specified. The mockups' serif headings are *not* adopted, because the written brief says "clean sans-serif". A serif display font for page titles could be added later as a single token change.
- **Sponsored accent:** the palette's Tea Green (`#DEF2C8`) with dark olive text marks the Sponsored pill and a faint card tint. It's distinct from organic posts but calm, and it doesn't compete with the brand pink.

Mockup features with no backend behind them (stories row, Messages, AI Assistant, "Creations", Media/Likes tabs) are **not built**. The scope is templates only, and fake UI would mislead people.

## Layout architecture
```
base.html                      <html>, <head> (Inter, Tailwind, @theme tokens, components), messages slot, {% block body %}
├── layouts/public.html        centered, branded; top bar (logo · Log in · Sign up); atmosphere gradient
├── layouts/social.html        REGULAR_USER
│     lg+:  [ sidebar 16rem ][ main max-w-2xl ][ right rail 20rem (xl+) ]
│     <lg:  sticky top bar (logo, account menu) · main · fixed bottom nav (Feed · People · Post · Profile)
└── layouts/portal.html        ADVERTISER
      lg+:  [ fixed sidebar 16rem ][ top header (title · company · menu) / content ]
      <lg:  top header + <details> menu with the same nav links
```
- **Active navigation** comes from `request.resolver_match.view_name`, which needs no view changes.
- **Page titles and actions** are template blocks (`{% block page_title %}`, `{% block page_actions %}`).
- The **mobile bottom nav** is `fixed bottom-0` with `pb-[env(safe-area-inset-bottom)]`. Main content gets matching bottom padding.
- **No JavaScript:** account and mobile menus are `<details>/<summary>`. They work without scripts and are accessible.

## Design tokens (Tailwind v4 `@theme`, in `base.html`)
- `--font-sans: "Inter", ui-sans-serif, system-ui…` (Google Fonts: Inter 400/500/600/700).
- `--color-brand-50…900`: a ramp around **`#E0266F`** (600), used for primary actions, active state, and focus.
- `--color-sage-50…900`: a ramp through the palette: 50 `#F6F9F3` (page), 100 `#EEF5E6`, 200 `#DEF2C8`, 300 `#C5DAC1`, 400 `#BCD0C7`, 500 `#A9B2AC`, 600 `#898980`, 700 `#5E625C`, 800 `#3C403B`, 900 `#232622` (body text). **Text contrast:** body `sage-900` on white is about 15:1; secondary `sage-700` is about 6:1 (≥ 4.5 AA); `sage-600` is only for large or decorative text.
- Radii: cards `rounded-2xl`, controls `rounded-xl`, pills `rounded-full`. Shadows: `shadow-sm` plus a custom soft shadow on cards, stronger on hover.
- The component layer (`@layer components`) defines `.card`, `.btn`, `.btn-primary`, `.btn-secondary`, `.btn-ghost`, `.btn-danger`, `.field`, `.input`, `.chip`, `.nav-link`, `.nav-link-active`, `.table`. The **existing class names (`btn`, `btn-primary`, `btn-secondary`, `form-stack`) keep working**, so nothing breaks during the migration.

## Tooling decision: Tailwind via CDN (kept)
| Option | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **Tailwind v4 browser CDN** (current) | **Kept.** No build step, and `@theme`/`@apply` work inside `<style type="text/tailwindcss">`. The Gardener said the CDN is acceptable. |
| Compiled build (Node is installed: v24) | Better for production (smaller CSS, no runtime compilation), but it adds `package.json`, a build command, and a compiled asset to keep in sync. **Deferred to pre-production.** The tokens and component layer move over unchanged. |

## Forms
Rendering goes through `components/form.html` → `components/form_field.html` instead of `form.as_p`. Inputs, selects, textareas, and file and date inputs are styled by CSS rules under `.field` (a neutral border, a brand focus ring, and a red border plus message on error via `aria-invalid`/an error class on the wrapper). **`django-widget-tweaks` was rejected:** it needs an `INSTALLED_APPS` settings change, which is outside the templates-only scope, and the CSS approach styles every widget type without touching Python.

## New template tags (UI-only; allowed by the brief)
| Library | Tag / filter | Purpose | Queries |
|---|---|---|---|
| `accounts/templatetags/ui.py` | `avatar_tone` filter, `initials` filter | Pick a deterministic avatar color from `user.pk`; show 1–2 initials | 0 |
| `posts/templatetags/social_widgets.py` | `{% who_to_follow %}` (inclusion) | Up to 3 regular users the viewer doesn't follow, not themselves, most followed first | **1** |
| | `{% trending_posts %}` (inclusion) | Up to 3 posts from the last 7 days with the most likes (≥ 1), authors joined in | **1** |

Both widgets run in the social layout's right rail on every social page. Each is **one bounded query** (`LIMIT 3`), so page query counts go up by a **constant 2** and stay independent of content. The pinned numbers in `posts/tests/test_queries.py` and `ads/tests/test_feed_queries.py` will be updated by exactly +2, with a comment. The N+1 guarantee is unchanged.

## Tests and the "templates only" boundary
Production Python (models, views, URLs, forms, settings) is **not touched.** Test files are touched in two narrow ways, both flagged:
1. **Decoupling commit first** (before any redesign): around 6 assertions that match exact markup (`"♥ Liked · 1"`, `">Follow</button>"`, `">Unfollow</button>"`) are switched to stable `data-testid` hooks, which are added to the **current** templates in the same commit. The suite stays green, and the behavior asserted is identical. After that, the redesign can change markup freely.
   - One test, *"own profile has no follow button"*, would otherwise fail for a *correct* reason: the new "Who to follow" rail legitimately contains Follow buttons for **other** users. It's re-targeted to the profile header's follow control.
2. **Query constants +2** for the right-rail widgets (above).

## Verification approach
- The full test suite runs after every commit, and a commit only happens if it passes.
- **Visual check:** headless Chrome (installed) screenshots every key page at **390 × 844 (phone)** and **1440 × 900 (desktop)**, for a visitor, a regular user, and an advertiser. The pages are rendered through Django's test client with a scratch script (kept outside the repo), and I review the screenshots.
- A diff check at the end confirms that no `models.py`, `views.py`, `urls.py`, `forms.py`, or settings changed.

## Risks & Open Questions
**Direction choices to confirm at Rendezvous:**
1. **Brand color is magenta-pink `#E0266F`** from the mockups, and the neutrals are the sage palette. The alternative would be using a palette green as the primary, but none of those swatches is vibrant enough for buttons.
2. **Inter only.** No serif headings, per the written brief rather than the mockups.
3. **Mockup-only features aren't built** (stories, messages, AI assistant, media tabs).
4. **Right rail widgets:** "Who to follow" ranks by follower count. "Trending" means the most-liked posts from the last 7 days.
5. **Mobile bottom nav:** Feed · People · **Post** (jumps to the composer on the feed) · Profile. Logout is in the top-bar account menu.

**Technical risks**
- The CDN Tailwind compiles in the browser, so there may be a brief flash of unstyled content on first load. That's acceptable for development, and the compiled build is planned pre-production.
- Without JavaScript, the composer's image picker shows the native file input, styled but without a preview thumbnail.
- The right-rail widgets add 2 queries to every social page (constant).
